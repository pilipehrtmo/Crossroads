How your document is handled

  • It is stored in private storage that no web page links to. Only site admins can open it, and every time they do is logged.
  • It is used only to check that you study here, never shown on your profile or to other students.
  • The file is deleted 14 days after approval or 30 days after a rejection. We keep only the decision, the date and what kind of document it was.
